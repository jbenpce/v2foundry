# Phase 0 — doctrine and one diagnostic

**Window:** 0 to 3 months from the day hours are formally allocated.
**Goal:** learn who turns up and what they are actually frightened of, at the
lowest possible cost, while establishing the voice.

Phase 0 is not a launch. It is instrumentation.

## What ships

### 1. The doctrine — GenXAlpha

Eight to twelve published pieces, one voice, no paywall, no email gate on the
writing itself.

The argument, in order:

1. The ladder broke at the bottom, not the top.
2. The junior work was the tuition, and it is gone.
3. A career was never the whole problem: four sheets, coupled.
4. Ownership and judgment are what stay scarce.
5. What wealthy families have always done, and why nobody does it for you.
6. Energy is the scarce resource, not time or money.
7. The people being pushed out hold what the people coming in cannot buy.
8. What to actually do this quarter.

**Standing rule:** every piece ends with something the reader can do on Monday.
No exceptions, including the ones that feel important. This is the guard against
doom drift, and it is worthless the first time it is waived.

### 2. The diagnostic

One public tool. Five minutes. No account required to see the result; an email
address is required only to save it and to receive the next quarter's re-read.

**Inputs — roughly twenty questions across the four sheets**

- *Work:* role, share of weekly time by task type, how much output is reviewed by
  someone senior, whether any of it is owned, how many people would vouch for
  your judgment unprompted.
- *Capital:* months of runway, savings rate, debt, whether anything compounds,
  which wrappers are in use.
- *Body:* sleep hours and consistency, training days per week, alcohol, how often
  you feel physically capable of a hard day.
- *Mind:* longest daily block of unbroken attention, whether you can name what
  you are building toward, who you talk to before a hard decision.

Plus one free-text field: **"What are you most worried about?"** That field is the
actual product research. Everything else is scaffolding around it.

**Outputs — three things, no more**

1. **Position.** Three numbers, stated plainly: task-surface exposure, trust
   premium, runway in months. Not gamified, not softened. A person who is in
   trouble should be able to tell.
2. **Energy Index.** A composite across the four sheets, with the weakest sheet
   named explicitly.
3. **Three moves.** One per weakest sheet, specific, achievable this quarter. Not
   a list of twenty. The restraint is the product.

**Explicitly out of scope in Phase 0:** accounts, payments, the Circle, the
quarterly review loop, mobile, any conversational interface, and the platform
itself. If it needs the AEGISfo spine, it belongs in Phase 2.

## First users

Users one, two and three are the founder's three children. If the tool does not
survive them, it will not survive strangers, and they will be considerably more
honest about it than a landing page test.

This is also the first place to notice the constraint that shapes the whole
concept: a parent cannot mentor their own child. Watch how the advice lands
coming from a screen rather than from you.

## What Phase 0 measures

Falsifiable, recorded weekly, reviewed at the end of the window.

| Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Completions | Whether the diagnostic is short enough to finish |
| Completion rate from start | Where the questions lose people |
| Email save rate | Whether the result was worth keeping |
| Free-text responses | The only source of genuine product direction |
| Return rate at 90 days | Whether the Energy Index is a reason to come back |
| Cohort interest | Count of people who say yes to a paid ten-week cohort |

## Exit criteria

**Proceed to Phase 1** when the free-text responses cluster into a recognisable
set of problems, and enough people have expressed interest in a paid cohort to
fill twenty seats twice over. Interest is not payment, so discount it heavily and
still require the number.

**Stop or rethink** if completions are healthy but the free-text is generic, or
if the responses show the audience arriving is 50-plus rather than 25 to 35. The
second outcome is not a failure. It means the supply side found the doctrine
first, which is useful information and a different build order.

## Open decisions blocking Phase 0

- Geography. UK-first or global. This decides how specific the Capital sheet
  questions can be, and specificity is most of the value.
- Where the writing lives. Own domain, or an existing publishing platform for
  distribution first and ownership later.
- Whether the diagnostic is a static page or needs a backend on day one. Prefer
  static until the return-visit loop is proved.
