For thirty years the deal was simple enough that nobody needed to write it down.

Do the junior work. Sit close enough to watch judgment form. Climb.

The junior work was never the point. It was the tuition.

## What disappeared

Models now do first-pass research, drafting, comps, boilerplate code, deck-building and summarisation at close to zero marginal cost. The tasks are gone. The apprenticeship that lived *inside* those tasks went with them.

The rungs above still demand exactly the judgment those tasks used to manufacture. That is the break. Not “AI took jobs” as a slogan — the specific mechanism by which young people used to become people who could be trusted with harder work.

| The deal, until recently | The deal now |
| --- | --- |
| Partner / Principal | Partner / Principal |
| Director | Director |
| Manager | Manager |
| Analyst | *(no clear route up)* |
| Trainee | ~~Analyst~~ |
| | ~~Trainee~~ |

This is not the Gen X displacement story, and it should not be sold as one. A fifty-five year old made redundant still has equity, a pension, a network, and thirty years of pattern recognition. A twenty-eight year old has none of those — and is competing against people who do, on ground that keeps moving.

> One generation lost its job. The next one lost the way in.

## What this is not

It is not a reason to panic-quit, chase a hustle, or declare careers dead. Careers did not die. The **entry tuition** died. That is narrower, and more useful, because it points at what to rebuild.

Ownership and judgment are what stay scarce when execution gets cheap. Everything else is noise until you can name your position on those two.

## What the Foundry is for

Wealthy families have run a whole-life operating model for two hundred years: a long horizon, a capital plan, trusted advisers, governance, and a formal review every quarter. Nobody runs that for someone with an unstable income and fifty years ahead of them.

That gap is the product. Four sheets — work, capital, body, mind — read together, not as separate self-help lanes. An Energy Index instead of a vanity metric. A short list of moves, then a review like an investment committee on your own life.

Doctrine stays free. The system is where you go to stop arguing with the fog.

If you are twenty-five to thirty-five, geographically mobile, English-speaking, and already suspicious that the ladder you were sold does not exist under your feet — you are not late. You are early to the honest map.
