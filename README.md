# V2 Foundry

A holistic system for rebuilding a life — work, capital, body and mind — for the
first generation whose entry-level work was automated before they finished
doing it.

**The place is the Foundry. The thing you build there is your 2.x.**

- **Who it is for:** geographically mobile English-speaking 25 to 35 year olds.
  Remote workers, people on European inbound regimes, Americans and Brits who
  left. Their career is already not a ladder, so the thesis needs no selling.
- **Doctrine:** GenXAlpha — the writing, free, always.
- **Home:** v2foundry.com
- **Status:** Phase 0, pre-build. Nothing has shipped.

## The one-paragraph version

The career ladder did not break at the top, it broke at the bottom. The junior
work was never the point; it was the tuition, and models do it now. Meanwhile a
career was never the whole problem: work, money, body and mind are coupled, and
fixing one while the other three are broken does not hold. So take the operating
model wealthy families have used for two hundred years — four balance sheets, a
long horizon, real advisers and a quarterly review — and give it to somebody who
has no wealth yet, at the age where it still compounds.

## The four sheets

| Sheet | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Work** | Task-surface exposure, trust premium, ownership, compounding vs decaying skills |
| **Capital** | Runway, buffer, wrappers, structure, the architecture nobody teaches |
| **Body** | Sleep, food, training, substances — the base every other sheet draws on |
| **Mind** | Attention, philosophy, relationships, the story you are running on |

The Capital sheet is a universal spine plus a thin **jurisdiction pack**: the
questions and scoring are identical everywhere, and only the named instruments
change. Three packs at launch, being the United States, the United Kingdom, and a
generic European fallback.

The **Energy Index** is the composite reading across all four, scored quarterly.
Energy, not time or money, is the scarce resource at this age, and it is the only
number that moves when the whole system improves.

## The three layers

1. **Doctrine** — GenXAlpha. The writing. Free. Builds worldview and trust.
2. **Foundry** — the system. Position, Energy Index, a short list of moves, and a
   quarterly review run like an investment committee on your own life.
3. **Circle** — the missing apprenticeship. Judgment transferred from displaced
   senior operators to people who cannot buy it at any price.
